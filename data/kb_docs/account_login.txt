PulseTrack Account and Login Policy

Account creation
- A PulseTrack account requires a valid email address and password, or
  sign-in via Google or Apple SSO.
- Each account may be linked to one PulseTrack device at a time; a device
  must be unpaired before it can be linked to a different account.

Password reset
- Users can reset their password from the app's login screen via
  "Forgot Password," which sends a reset link valid for 60 minutes.
- If the reset email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check spam/junk
  folders before contacting support, since delivery delays are the most
  common cause.

Two-factor authentication (2FA)
- 2FA is optional but recommended, and can be enabled under
  Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication using an authenticator
  app.
- If a user is locked out due to a lost 2FA device, support can verify
  identity and disable 2FA after confirming the account email and the
  last 4 digits of the payment method on file.

Common login issues
- "Invalid credentials" errors are most often caused by an outdated app
  version; users should update to the latest version before retrying.
- Accounts are temporarily locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed login
  attempts as a security measure.
- Users who signed up via Google/Apple SSO cannot log in with a password
  and must use the original SSO provider.

Account deletion
- Users can request account deletion from Account > Privacy > Delete
  Account, or by emailing support@pulsetrack.example.
- Deletion is permanent after a 30-day grace period, during which the
  request can be cancelled by logging back in.
