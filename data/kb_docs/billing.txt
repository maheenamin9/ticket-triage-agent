PulseTrack Billing Policy

PulseTrack offers two subscription tiers for the companion app: PulseTrack
Free and PulseTrack Plus ($7.99/month or $69/year). The device itself is a
one-time purchase and is not billed on a subscription.

Billing cycle
- Plus subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each month (or
  year) as the original purchase date.
- Invoices are emailed to the account's registered email address within
  24 hours of each charge and are also available under Account > Billing
  History in the app.

Payment methods
- We accept major credit/debit cards and PayPal.
- To update a payment method, go to Account > Billing > Payment Methods.
  There is no need to cancel and re-subscribe.

Failed payments
- If a renewal payment fails, we retry automatically for 3 days. During
  this window Plus features remain active.
- After 3 failed attempts, the account is downgraded to PulseTrack Free
  and the user is notified by email.

Refunds
- Annual Plus subscriptions are eligible for a full refund within 14 days
  of purchase if requested via support@pulsetrack.example.
- Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once a billing cycle has
  started, but users can cancel at any time to stop future charges.
- Device purchases follow the 30-day return policy described in the
  shipping and returns document.

Cancellation
- Plus can be cancelled anytime from Account > Billing > Cancel
  Subscription. Access continues until the end of the current paid period.
- Cancelling does not delete workout history or account data.
