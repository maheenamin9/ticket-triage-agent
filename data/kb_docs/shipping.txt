PulseTrack Shipping and Returns Policy

Order processing
- Orders placed before 2pm ET ship the same business day; orders placed
  after 2pm ET ship the next business day.
- Orders are not processed on weekends or public holidays.

Shipping options and delivery times (domestic)
- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days, free on orders over $50.
- Expedited shipping: 1-2 business days, flat rate $12.99.
- International shipping is available to select countries and typically
  takes 7-14 business days; customs fees may apply and are the
  responsibility of the customer.

Tracking
- A tracking number is emailed automatically as soon as a label is
  created, usually within a few hours of the order shipping.
- Tracking can also be viewed under Account > Orders > Order Details.
- If a tracking number shows no movement for more than 5 business days,
  contact support@pulsetrack.example so we can open a carrier trace.

Lost or damaged packages
- If a package arrives damaged, keep all packaging and photograph the
  item; contact support within 7 days of delivery for a free replacement.
- If a package is marked delivered but not received, we ask customers to
  check with neighbors/building management and wait 48 hours before we
  file a carrier investigation.

Returns
- PulseTrack devices can be returned unused or used within 30 days of
  delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Return shipping is free using the prepaid label available under
  Account > Orders > Start a Return.
- Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after the returned item is
  received and inspected.
